Fun Stuff

Money Over Time

Running dollar total for every participant, week by week. Hover for exact amounts, click a name in the legend to hide or show a line, and double-click a name to isolate it.

Weekly Results

Each cell is one person’s dollar result for one week. Green means they made money, red means they lost money, and gray means they broke even. Participants are sorted with the current leader on top. Hover for the exact amount.

Where the Money Comes From

Each person’s season total split into base picks, bonus picks, and missed-pick penalties. The diamond marks the net total, so you can see who is making money on bonus picks and who is losing it.

Win Percentage Over Time

Each person’s cumulative win rate across the picks they actually made, with pushes counted as wins. This leaves out the dollar swings from bonus picks, so it shows who is picking well rather than who got lucky with a bonus. The dotted line is 50%, the break-even point against the spread. Early in the season these lines swing a lot because there are few picks behind them.

Win Probability

(This is a work in progress. Do not read into it much.)

The chance each person finishes the season with the most money, from simulating the rest of the season 10,000 times. Hover for each person’s current total and expected final total.
